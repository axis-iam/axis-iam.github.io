## Users and purpose

This is the public developer documentation for Axis Vertex, an Application-centric identity platform.
Readers connect applications, add sign-in, protect APIs and operate enterprise integrations.
English and Simplified Chinese have equivalent guides. Callable SDK references share their source provenance.

The homepage helps readers choose their next task. Article pages prioritize navigation, readable code, accurate
configuration and clear security boundaries. The documentation does not imply that an SDK is published merely
because its source is public.
